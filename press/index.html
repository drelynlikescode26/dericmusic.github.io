Short Bio

Deric (Drelyn Walker) is an Atlanta recording artist and songwriter working across R&B and melodic rap. His catalog includes the Mood Swings & Melodies series and singles including Dank and Dog Sh*t. His music has appeared on Spotify and Apple Music editorial playlists, including Spotify's Fresh Finds.
