Deric is the artist name of Drelyn Walker, a singer and songwriter from Atlanta. His records move between R&B and melodic rap.

His catalog includes the Mood Swings & Melodies series and singles including Dank and Dog Sh*t. Explore the releases on the Music page and watch the videos on the Videos page.
